One DTC skincare brand I worked with saw carousel posts average 2.8x more saves than Reels, while Reels drove more profile visits. The combination maximised both reach and conversion.

Format Decision Framework:

   Use carousels for: Teaching content, data/statistics, frameworks, actionable tips
   Use Reels for: Brand awareness, behind-the-scenes, trending topics, product demos
   Track saves, shares, and reach over 30 days to determine what works for your specific audience [...] Real Example:

 A skincare brand came to us posting daily Reels with perfect execution - trending audio, strong hooks, proper hashtags. But 38% watch time meant invisible reach. We shifted them to carousel-first strategy. Result: 74% completion rates and 340% more qualified leads through DMs.

The key insight: Instagram rewards deeper engagement over entertainment value for business accounts. Carousels naturally drive the engagement signals the algorithm now prioritises.

Action Plan: [...] I’d use both, but not for the same job.

Reels are better for reach. They get shown to people who don’t follow you yet, so they’re good for growth.

Carousels are better for saves, comments, and trust. They work well for tips, breakdowns, before/after, mistakes, checklists, etc.

So I’d do something like:

   2-3 Reels a week to reach new people.
   1-2 carousels a week to keep them engaged.

If your account is still small, lean more into Reels first.
