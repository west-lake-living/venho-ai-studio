Organic Reach Rate (ORR) is the percentage of your followers (page likes) who see your posts WITHOUT spending money on ads.

Post ORR = Organic Reach divided by Followers

Profile ORR = Average of all Post ORR’s during the given time interval (ie: last month) [...] During your analysis you may see posts with Organic Reach Rates over 100%! Yes, this is possible – all it means is that your post is going viral and you are reaching people outside of your followers. On smaller profiles this is actually quite common. Don’t believe the hype – organic reach is definitely not dead on Facebook!
