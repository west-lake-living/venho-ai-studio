## Single images lose the fastest

For its 2026 Instagram study, Metricool compared around 24.4 million posts from 375,118 accounts, January and February 2025 against the same period in 2026. For single image posts, reach fell by 21.96 percent, interactions by 25.41 percent and the engagement rate by 45.98 percent. In the same data set reels achieve more than four times as many interactions as single images, and carousels generate nine times more saves than single image posts. ## FAQ

What brings more reach, reels or carousels?

Reels. In the Buffer analysis of more than four million Instagram posts, reels achieved 36 percent more reach than carousels and 125 percent more than single images. The reason lies in distribution: reels run through recommendation surfaces to people with no follower relationship, carousels mostly in the feed of the existing audience.

Reels vs carousel is not an either/or question but a question of goal: according to a Buffer analysis, reels achieve 36 percent more reach than carousels, while carousels get 12 percent more engagement and, according to Metricool, nine times more saves than single images. Reels bring in people who do not know you yet, carousels go deeper with the people who are already there.
