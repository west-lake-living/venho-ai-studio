## Reels win reach. Carousels win engagement.

Start with the split, because it holds up across every serious dataset. Reels reach more people. Carousels get more out of the people they reach. [...] Buffer’s analysis of more than 4 million Instagram posts found Reels pull about 36 percent more reach than carousels and roughly 125 percent more than single images. Turn to engagement and the order flips: carousels earn around 109 percent more engagement per person reached than Reels, and even single images beat Reels by about 34 percent on that measure.
