Try publishing different types of content. In addition to posts with photos and videos, you can create dynamic content with Stories and Reels. These formats can sometimes feel more informal and engaging and help people connect with your business. [...] Improve post quality. Make sure you post content your audience wants to see. Your reach is driven by how much your audience values the content you post. This is based on positive interactions and engagement with the content you post, such as link clicks, comments, shares, saves and reactions. Don’t post content that includes:
       Clickbait 
       Engagement bait 
       Watchbait 
       Links to low quality web experiences 
       Low quality video 
       Misleading experiences [...] Start advertising. You can also improve your reach by boosting a post or creating an ad. Advertising is a direct way to reach new customers and potentially convert your existing audience into customers. You can apply Advantage+ creative to your ad, for example, which will then optimize your images and videos to versions that your audience is most likely to interact with. Similarly, Advantage+ audience can help you find your audience.
