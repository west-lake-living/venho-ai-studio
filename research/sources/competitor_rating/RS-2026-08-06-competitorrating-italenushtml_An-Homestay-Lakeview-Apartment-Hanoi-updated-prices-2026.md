### About this property
**Comfortable Accommodations:** An Homestay Lakeview Apartment in Hanoi offers family rooms with air-conditioning, private bathrooms, and city views. Each room includes a dining table, work desk, and seating area. **Modern Amenities:** Guests enjoy free WiFi, daily housekeeping, express check-in and check-out, and a balcony with lake views. Additional amenities include a kitchen with a stovetop, electric kettle, and kitchenware. **Convenient Location:** Located 13 mi from Noi Bai International Airport, the homestay is near attractions such as One Pillar Pagoda (1.6 mi), Vincom Center Nguyen Chi Thanh (1.8 mi), and West Lake (2.4 mi).
Couples in particular like the location – they rated it **9.0** for a two-person trip.
