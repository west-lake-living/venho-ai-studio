The Imperial Citadel of Thong Long is a must-visit historic site in Hanoi also known as the Hanoi Citadel. I love wandering through the imperial buildings which were once the site of the political centre of Vietnam for 13 consecutive centuries. You can also glimpse artefacts and items dating back to between the 6th and 20th centuries! Located in Ba Dinh District, entrance to the Imperial Citadel of Thang Long is priced at VND 30,000 and it is open from 08:00 to 17:00 every day except Monday. [...] How many days do you need in Hanoi? I’d recommend three to four days minimum to explore the Old Quarter, visit key temples, enjoy the food scene and do at least one day trip. If you have a week, you can add a Halong Bay cruise and visit Ninh Binh and the Perfume Pagoda without feeling rushed. [...] One of the more unexpected experiences in Hanoi is X Space Immersive, an indoor digital art attraction tucked inside Vincom Mega Mall Royal City. It’s a refreshing contrast to the temples, coffee shops and bustling streets that define much of the city. The space combines projection mapping, interactive light displays and themed environments inspired by everything from the ocean to outer space, giving you a completely different side of Hanoi.
