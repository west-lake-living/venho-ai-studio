October 20, 2025 - Rockstore (61 Mã Mây): Expanded in 2024 to include a rooftop area, this live rock music venue hosts performances five nights a week from local Vietnamese bands and traveling international acts. The Old Quarter is especially vibrant from Thursday through Sunday, when vehicular traffic is restricted in the evening hours (6:00 PM – 12:00 AM), creating a pedestrian-friendly atmosphere. According to the Hanoi Tourism Board, this area sees an average of 27,000 visitors nightly during peak season.
