The One Pillar Pagoda is another of Hanoi’s most iconic landmarks, and perhaps its most unique. Built in 1049, the pagoda was designed to resemble a lotus flower rising gracefully from the water. The Temple is located inside the secured area for the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum, so if you’re heading to one, make sure to visit both.

## Experiences and sights you can’t miss in Hanoi

### Visit X Space Immersive [...] Hanoi’s rooftops are some of the best places to escape the chaos below. There are so many great ones around Hoan Kiem Lake and West Lake. My favorites were Skyline Hanoi and Solar Sky Bar in Old Quarter and The Summit Bar around West Lake. Come for sunset or later in the evening to see Hanoi city all litt up.

Views from Solar Sky Bar

### Hoan Kiem Lake & Ngoc Son Temple [...] If you want something completely different from the Old Quarter, temples, markets and museums, X Space Immersive is a fun and modern experience to add to your Hanoi itinerary.

It’s an indoor digital art attraction inside Vincom Mega Mall Royal City, with projection mapping, LED rooms, light effects, interactive sensors and themed spaces inspired by fantasy worlds, nature, the ocean and outer space.
