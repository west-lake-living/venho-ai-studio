# VIEW

Visualizing Indicators of Education for the World (VIEW) provides estimates of two flagship indicators: out-of-school and completion rates. Using multiple data sources in an efficient and transparent way, it calculates time series by country and region and addresses challenges of timeliness and consistency commonly associated with administrative and survey data.
