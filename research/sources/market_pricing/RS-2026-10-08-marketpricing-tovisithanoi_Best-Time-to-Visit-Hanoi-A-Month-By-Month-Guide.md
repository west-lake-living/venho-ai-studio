The best months to visit Hanoi for sightseeing are March and October. The weather is pleasant during these shoulder season months, and the crowds are manageable. Visit Hanoi landmarks like the Temple of Literature, Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum, and the Hanoi Opera House without the peak tourist rush.

## Hanoi throughout the seasons

### Spring in Hanoi (March to May) [...] October and November are widely considered the best months to visit Hanoi thanks to cooler temperatures, lower humidity, and clearer skies. Spring, especially March and April, is also popular for mild weather and blooming trees around the city’s lakes and boulevards. [...] Hanoi’s rainy season typically runs from May to September, with July and August seeing the heaviest rainfall. Expect hot, humid weather and short but intense downpours, though the countryside around Hanoi is at its greenest during this time.

The cheapest time to visit Hanoi is usually during the hot and rainy summer months from May to August, when fewer international travelers visit. Flights and hotels are often more affordable outside major holidays like Tet and Christmas.
