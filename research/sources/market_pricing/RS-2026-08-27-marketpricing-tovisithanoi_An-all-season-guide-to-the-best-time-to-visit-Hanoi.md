### What is the best month to visit Hanoi?

October and November are widely considered the best months to visit Hanoi thanks to cooler temperatures, lower humidity, and clearer skies. Spring, especially March and April, is also popular for mild weather and blooming trees around the city’s lakes and boulevards.
